Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026. This policy covers the MarginMark Chrome extension.

What we collect

  • Account email and password hash (password is never stored in the extension).
  • Subscription status from Stripe (no full card numbers).
  • Privacy-safe usage event names and coarse counts. Telemetry excludes email, raw URLs, product titles, statement rows, and cost data.

What we do not collect

  • TikTok passwords or session cookies.
  • Payment card PAN/CVC (handled by Stripe Checkout).
  • SKU records, product costs, imported statements, creator (affiliate) order exports, your Shop Performance Score, or other seller financial data. Costs, profit results, creator results, and the weekly recap stay on the device.

Optional trending

Trending is not offered in this version. If a later version offers it and you turn it on, MarginMark sends anonymous public product activity: product id, price, approximate sold count, and rating. It does not include your account, costs, or profit. Aggregated trends (products checked by 5 or more sellers) may be shown in MarginMark and published on plainsmansoftware.com. Raw events are deleted after 90 days. Turning the toggle off stops sending immediately. Scrubbed error reports (version, surface, and a hash of the message) may be sent so outages can be fixed.

Storage

Settings, SKU records, costs, imported statements, imported creator orders, weekly recap snapshots (the last 12 weeks), and your Shop Performance Score stay on your computer in Chrome extension storage. On Seller Center's Account Health page, MarginMark reads only the score number so it can warn you when it drops; it reads nothing else on that page. Auth uses a bearer token in extension storage. The backend stores account email, password hash, subscription status, and privacy-safe telemetry.

Sign-in

You can create an account with email or with Google, Facebook, or TikTok. Those providers receive the sign-in request. MarginMark stores the provider subject id and your email.

Processors

Stripe processes payments. Fly.io hosts the API. Neon hosts the database. Anthropic may summarize public review text if that feature is offered and turned on (it is not in this version). New Relic receives operational telemetry. Costs and profit stay in the extension.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while the account exists. Deleting an account cancels the Stripe subscription, deletes the Stripe customer, removes the user id from telemetry, and deletes support tickets older than 30 days. Anonymous trend events are deleted after 90 days. Settings → Delete my account does this. You can also email support@plainsmansoftware.com.

Limited Use

MarginMark's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
