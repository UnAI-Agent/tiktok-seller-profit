Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026. This is a launch placeholder — replace with your legal counsel’s final policy before Chrome Web Store publication.

What we collect

  • Account email and password hash (password is never stored in the extension).
  • Subscription status from Stripe (no full card numbers).
  • Optional support tickets you submit.
  • Product SKU records you sync, stored in the extension and, when signed in, on our API.

What we do not collect

  • TikTok passwords or session cookies.
  • Payment card PAN/CVC (handled by Stripe Checkout).

Storage

Settings and SKUs live in chrome.storage.local. Auth uses a bearer token in extension storage. The backend stores accounts in a database you operate (SQLite in alpha, PostgreSQL in production).
